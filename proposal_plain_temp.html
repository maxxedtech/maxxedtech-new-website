1. Executive Summary

Sarkin Mota Group has grown into a premier luxury brand encompassing high-end vehicle sales, bespoke interior transformations, and luxury apparel. To match your physical market reputation, we have designed a unified digital platform that brings all your business divisions under one prestigious online brand while giving management complete control over day-to-day operations.

The platform is structured around three core pillars:

  • Sarkin Mota Autos: A complete online showroom for brand-new and certified foreign-used luxury vehicles, featuring financing calculators, civil servant payment plans, trade-in valuations, and inspection scheduling.
  • Custom Interiors by Sarkin Mota: A dedicated showcase for VIP seat re-upholstery, starlight headliner installations, and executive office or residential styling.
  • Sarkin Mota Fabrics & Apparel: An exclusive preview and waitlist system designed to capture high-value customer interest ahead of your full collection launch.

2. How Customers Experience Your Brand Online (sarkinmota.ng)

Your public website is built to reflect a high-end luxury experience, making it effortless for clients to browse and engage with your business across both vehicles and custom styling:

  • Luxury Visual Identity: Designed with an elegant aesthetic tailored specifically to high-net-worth clients.
  • Smart Vehicle Filtering: Allows customers to instantly sort vehicles by make, model, year, price, and features.
  • Side-by-Side Comparison: Enables prospective buyers to evaluate specifications and pricing of multiple vehicles at the same time.
  • Trade-In & Vehicle Swaps: A simple upload tool where customers can submit details and photos of their current vehicle for a rapid appraisal.
  • Flexible Payment Calculators: Instantly computes down payments and monthly payment tenures for private buyers and civil servants, sending structured applications directly to your sales team via WhatsApp.
  • Inspection & Test Drive Booking: An online calendar system allowing clients to schedule visits to your showroom.
  • Interactive Before/After Styling Sliders: Allows prospective clients to swipe horizontally across visual transformations of custom vehicle interiors (such as VIP seating and starlight headliners) and executive architectural spaces.
  • Custom Consultation Desk: A structured intake form capturing specific project requirements and scheduling on-site appointments for luxury interiors.
  • Fabrics VIP Waitlist: An exclusive lead-capture system for seasonal fabric drops, preparing your audience for the upcoming e-commerce collection.

3. Key Business Benefits & Automation

  • Instant Customer Enquiries: Customer requests from the website are automatically organized and forwarded straight to your sales team's WhatsApp for faster closing.
  • Image Protection: All vehicle and interior photographs published online are automatically protected with the Sarkin Mota watermark to prevent unauthorized copying by competitors.
  • 24/7 Customer Assistance: An automated assistant helps answer common customer questions regarding pricing, locations, and financing criteria at any time of day.
  • Monthly Executive Insights: Management receives regular performance summaries detailing inventory turnover, high-demand vehicle models, and total lead volumes.

4. Implementation Roadmap

  • Phase 1: Foundation & Core Showrooms: Build the underlying system architecture and launch the public website (sarkinmota.ng) featuring the vehicle catalog, loan calculator, interior gallery, and fabrics waitlist, alongside the secure admin portal (admin.sarkinmota.ng).
  • Phase 2: Media & Automation Integration: Connect the automated photo protection system, set up direct WhatsApp sales routing, and activate customer assistance tools.
  • Phase 3: Fabrics E-Commerce Expansion: Launch the public fabric lookbook and direct ordering capabilities for waitlisted clients.
